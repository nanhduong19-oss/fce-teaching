## Practice 11 — A Shared Study Space

### Completed passage

### Item explanations

**17. BEHAVE → BEHAVIOUR** (noun)

BEHAVE → BEHAVIOUR (noun). 'complaints about noisy behaviour' requires a noun after the adjective 'noisy'. Context: “The teachers had received complaints about noisy behaviour in the library, where pupils were trying to work during lunch.” Common trap: behave (verb) and behavioural (adjective) cannot fill the noun position.

**18. CHOOSE → CHOICE** (noun)

CHOOSE → CHOICE (noun). 'given a choice between A and B' is a fixed pattern. Context: “At the first meeting, students were given a choice between creating a completely silent room and providing an area for group projects.” Common trap: choose is a verb; chosen is a participle.

CONCLUDE → CONCLUSION (noun). 'reach the conclusion that ...' expresses the group's final judgement. Context: “After listening to both sides, the committee reached the conclusion that a flexible room would be more useful.” Common trap: conclude is a verb, not a noun.

**20. CONSTRUCT → CONSTRUCTIVE** (adjective)

CONSTRUCT → CONSTRUCTIVE (adjective). 'constructive criticism' means useful feedback intended to improve a plan. Context: “A teacher offered constructive criticism of the original design, pointing out that the movable tables might block the entrance.” Common trap: construction refers to building and is a noun.

**21. CREATE → CREATIVITY** (noun)

CREATE → CREATIVITY (noun). 'show considerable creativity' needs an abstract noun for original ideas. Context: “Rather than abandon their idea, the students showed considerable creativity by designing two separate zones.” Common trap: creative is an adjective.

**22. DECIDE → DECISION** (noun)

DECIDE → DECISION (noun). 'make the final decision' requires a singular count noun. Context: “The head teacher made the final decision once the safety plan had been approved.” Common trap: decide is a verb; decisions is plural.

**23. DEVELOP → DEVELOPMENT** (noun)

DEVELOP → DEVELOPMENT (noun). 'during the development of the project' names a process. Context: “During the development of the project, volunteers discovered that younger and older pupils had different working habits.” Common trap: develop is a verb.

**24. DIFFERENT → DIFFERENCES** (noun)
